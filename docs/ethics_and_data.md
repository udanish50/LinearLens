# Ethics and data handling

The publication reports that the data used are confidential and that the human study received Western University's Non-Medical Research Ethics Board approval (Approval No. 125784).

This repository therefore does not include:

- participant-level survey responses;
- participant identifiers or demographic microdata;
- the confidential real-world energy datasets;
- private building information;
- research checkpoints trained on those data.

The synthetic examples are newly generated and contain no participant or building records.

If you apply the utilities to human-subject data, it is your responsibility to follow the consent, ethics, privacy, and data-governance requirements that apply to your study.
