# Selected published findings

This file records selected *reported* findings for context. They are not generated by the synthetic examples in this repository.

- Linear Lens was evaluated on ten real-world energy-consumption datasets spanning student residences, individual houses, and industrial/commercial buildings.
- The evaluation included MLP, LSTM, and Transformer models at widths 64, 128, 256, and 512.
- The publication reports that first-layer neurons were predominantly polysemantic (over 90% in aggregate patterns), while deeper layers were predominantly multimodal.
- A controlled human study included 400 participants.
- Reported internal-consistency values included Cronbach's alpha / McDonald's omega of 0.81/0.83 for the cognitive-load construct, 0.89/0.91 for trust & usability, 0.87/0.88 for comprehensibility, and 0.90/0.92 for actionability.
- Reported CFA fit indices included CFI 0.91, TLI 0.92, RMSEA 0.058, and SRMR 0.051.

For authoritative values, cite and consult the paper itself.
